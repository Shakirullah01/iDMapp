About

What is the iMD App?

One subscription, one login

The iMD App is a single medical learning hub. One iMD subscription gives you access to 45,000+ resources: question banks, textbooks, video lectures, clinical and drug references, and iMD AI. There are no extra fees per database.

Built for licensing exams

Content is organized for USMLE Step 1, Step 2 CK and Step 3, PLAB 1 and 2 (UKMLA), AMC MCQ and Clinical, MCCQE Part 1 and NAC, and MRCP, as well as everyday clinical practice. Browse the iMD resources library.

iPhone, Android, Windows and Mac

Install the Android APK, open iMD web on iPhone and iPad, or run the app on Windows and macOS. Step-by-step instructions are in the iMD App download guide, and login help is available if you get stuck.
