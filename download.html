Instructions by device

How to download the iMD App on your device

These are the current access and download routes for the iMD App. If your device is not listed, contact support before installing anything else.

Android

iMD App APK for Android phones and tablets

Download the iMD APK and open it from your notifications or Downloads folder. If Android asks for permission to install apps from your browser, allow it, then open the app and log in.

Download the iMD APK
iOS

iMD App on iPhone and iPad

Open iMD web in Safari and sign in with the details sent after activation. Tap Share and then Add to Home Screen to use iMD like an installed app.

Open iMD web
Windows

iMD App on Windows PC and laptop

Install an Android emulator such as MEmu, then open the iMD APK inside it. You can also use iMD web directly in your browser.

First-time setup

Install, activate and log in to the iMD App

Follow these three steps using the device instructions above.

  1. 01

    Choose a subscription plan

    Compare iMD App prices (6 months, 1 year or 2 years) and contact the team to arrange payment. Payment is handled manually.

  2. 02

    Download or open the iMD App

    Use the route listed for your device: the Android APK, iMD web on iPhone and iPad, or an emulator on Windows and Mac. Enter an activation code only if the app asks for one.

  3. 03

    Log in with your access details

    After activation, sign in with the username and password supplied by support. If you need help, see iMD App login help.
